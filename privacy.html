Privacy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Current prototype

SupportFlow AI is currently a static, browser-only prototype. It does not provide user accounts, advertising, behavioral tracking, form storage, or live external AI calls. The interactive demo uses fictional sample content and runs in your browser.

Pilot inquiries

If you contact us by email, we will use the information you provide only to respond and evaluate potential pilot fit. Please do not send customer data, credentials, confidential information, or sensitive personal information.

Hosting

This site is delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process limited technical information needed to serve and secure the site under its own terms and privacy practices.

Future product

Before collecting product data or activating a live AI workflow, SupportFlow AI will publish updated terms, explain applicable controls, and obtain appropriate agreement from pilot partners.
